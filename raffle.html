Good to know

Questions

How much are tickets?

$5 for 1 ticket or $10 for 3 tickets. Each ticket is one number from 1 to 120.

Do I get a ticket when I buy lashes?

Yes. Every DIY Cluster Lash Kit (Volume or Natural) bought during the giveaway comes with 1 free ticket. Other products don't include a ticket. We assign you an available number automatically and it goes straight into the draw.

When can I buy tickets?

Right now, until Sunday 29 November 2026 at 6:00 pm Sydney time, or until all 120 tickets sell out.

How are winners picked and contacted?

Three winning numbers are drawn at random from all sold tickets. Winners are revealed in a live draw at 7pm Sunday 29 November, with the video posted on TikTok and Instagram. Each winner is also called on the number given at checkout.

Can I choose my own numbers?

Yes. Pick any number that isn't greyed out. Once you pay, that number is yours.

Who can enter?

Everyone can enter.
