Questions

How much are tickets?

$5 for 1 ticket or $10 for 3 tickets. Each ticket is one number from 1 to 120.

Do I get a ticket when I buy lashes?

Yes. Every DIY lash kit bought during the draw comes with 1 free ticket. We assign you an available number and email it to you. Confirm: kits only, or every product?

When do tickets close?

Sunday 29 November 2026 at 6:00 pm Sydney time, or earlier if all 120 tickets sell out.

How are winners picked and contacted?

Three winning numbers are drawn at random from all sold tickets. Winners are contacted by phone call on the number given at checkout. Confirm draw date and whether it's drawn live on Instagram

Can I choose my own numbers?

Yes. Pick any number that isn't greyed out. Once you pay, that number is yours.

Who can enter?

Anyone 18 or over with a delivery address in Australia or New Zealand. Confirm
