The raffle is run by Lash Cartel Cosmetics. Contact us at Lashcartel.co@outlook.com.

Every paid order placed through the online shop at lashcartelcosmetics.com is automatically entered into the raffle. Each paid order counts as 1 entry. There are no tickets to buy and nothing extra to do.

No purchase is necessary. A free alternative method of entry is available by contacting us during the entry period.

3. Entry period

Entries open on 12 November 2026 and close on the draw date (to be announced).

Orders that are cancelled or fully refunded are not eligible. We may exclude entries that are fraudulent or that break these terms.

The prize and the draw date will be announced on this page before the draw. The winner will be drawn at random from all eligible entries.

How the winner is contacted will be announced with the draw date.

6. Your details

We use the name and email from your order only to run the raffle and contact the winner, in line with our Privacy Policy.
