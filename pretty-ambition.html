Meet Pretty Ambition — our pre-glued cluster lash set for effortless, customisable, salon-level glam at home. Soft, wispy clusters lift, define and open the eye with a lightweight, barely-there feel. No mascara, no strip-lash struggle, no gluey mess — just clean, custom volume that's unapologetically you.

Each set includes three lengths (10mm, 12mm, 14mm) so you can map your look exactly how you like it — natural on the inner corners, fuller on the outer edge. The pre-bonded band presses on close to your natural lash line with a thin, seamless finish that blends right in. Reusable with proper care.

Key Features

  • Pre-Glued Press-On Clusters — ready to wear, no separate glue needed.
  • Thin, Seamless Band — sits close to the lash line for a natural, invisible finish.
  • 3 Lengths · 10/12/14mm — build a custom, seamless lash map.
  • Lightweight & Wispy — natural definition that opens the eye, all-day comfort.
  • Precision Applicator Included — angled tweezer for easy, accurate placement.
  • Reusable — gentle care keeps them looking new, wear after wear.

How to Use

  • Map your look — shorter clusters for the inner corners, longer for the outer edge.
  • Using the tweezer, pick up a cluster and place it just above your natural lash line.
  • Press gently to set and hold for a few seconds.
  • Work along the lash line, then lightly press to blend for a seamless finish.
