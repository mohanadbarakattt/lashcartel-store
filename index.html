It's Easy

Lashes On in 4 Steps

Salon-worthy lashes at home — no experience needed, ready in minutes.

1

Curl Your Lashes

To achieve a good curl effect, curl your natural lashes with a lash curler.

2

Place Under

Our under-lash system sits beneath your natural lashes — gently position it close to the lash line.

3

Press & Set

Press lightly to set. Weightless, invisible band — no heavy strip, no irritation.

4

Wear & Reuse

Long-lasting hold that lasts for days. Store and reuse, wear after wear.
